STATELESS AGENT

You: hi im roger
Model: Hi Roger! 👋 How can I help you today?

You: whats my name
Model: I don’t know your name from this chat. If you tell me what you want to be called, I’ll use it.


The stateless agent sends each message to the model by itself. It does not
keep conversation history or save anything externally.

CONVERSATION AGENT

You: hi im roger
Model: Hi Roger! 👋 How can I help you today?
You: whats my name
Model: Your name is Roger.
You: quit

You: whats my name
Model: I don’t know your name from this chat. If you tell me what you want to be called, I’ll use it.


The conversation agent keeps messages in a Python list, so it remembers
within one session. When that list is cleared, the memory is gone.

RAG AGENT

You: what are the best and worst toppings at this restaurant

Model: **Best toppings:** The toppings from the restaurant’s **“Experimental”** menu—e.g., the **maple-glazed brussels sprouts with pancetta and smoked gouda**—because reviewers say they “sound weird but it was incredible,” and the place “isn’t afraid to take risks.”
**Worst toppings:** The toppings in the meal where the **toppings slide right off**—those where the **sauce-to-cheese ratio** is off, causing toppings to **fall off on the first bite** (leaving basically “dough with sauce” and a separate pile of cheese/toppings).
Retrieved context:
Innovative toppings Their 'Experimental' menu section is amazing. Just tried one with maple-glazed brussels sprouts, pancetta, and smoked gouda - sounds weird but it was incredible. They're not afraid to take risks.
Perfect crust-to-topping ratio Some places overload toppings so the crust gets soggy, others are too sparse. This place gets it perfect - enough toppings to get some in every bite, but not so much that the structural integrity is compromised.
Mediocre at best Nothing terrible but nothing special either. The crust was okay, toppings were standard, and service was fine. It's the kind of place you go when you're in the area, but wouldn't make a special trip for.
Toppings slide right off The ratio of sauce to cheese was so off that all the toppings slid off with the first bite. Ended up eating what was essentially dough with sauce while the cheese and toppings formed a pile on my plate.
Suspiciously uniform toppings All the vegetable toppings were cut in precisely identical shapes and sizes, suggesting they came pre-cut from a food service provider rather than being prepared fresh in-house as claimed. Tasted fine but felt deceived by the marketing.

The RAG agent retrieves relevant restaurant reviews from the external vector store and gives those
reviews to the model as context. This represents external knowledge retrieval, similar to looking something up rather than remembering it personally.

--------------------

SUMMARY:
Stateless: forgets immediately.
Conversation: remembers during one session only.
DynamoDB memory: remembers a user's conversations across sessions.
RAG: retrieves information from an external knowledge source.
